About the project

Built for the college canteen problem

During the short break, hundreds of students crowd the canteen. The manual system forces students to queue twice — once to order and once to pay — while staff count cash by hand, have no record of daily sales, and only discover stock-outs when a student asks.

Smart Canteen Management System digitises the entire flow: students order and pay online, staff manage incoming orders from a dashboard, inventory updates automatically with every order, and sales analytics replace the register book.
